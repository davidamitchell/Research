# 2026-10-02 -- Research loop completion (ideal-internal-platform-product-standard)

**Completed:**
- `Research/completed/2026-10-02-ideal-internal-platform-product-standard.md` — full research cycle (backlog → in-progress → reviewing → completed) for "Beyond the Thinnest Viable Platform (TVP) floor, what observable criteria and operating practices define a high-performing internal platform built and managed as a product under Team Topologies, and how can they be synthesised into a practical assessment standard?" Assembles a three-layer assessable standard: six capability dimensions (Microsoft Learn capability model, aligned with the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) maturity model), a combined logs-based-plus-self-reported measurement approach (DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA) 2025 guidance plus the Satisfaction and well-being, Performance, Activity, Communication and collaboration, and Efficiency and flow (SPACE) framework), and a check-track-level-certification scoring mechanism adapted from Spotify Backstage's Soundcheck. Adds adoption-mode classification (voluntary/defaulted/mandated/migrated/inherited), Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort (RICE) backlog scoring, the Now-Next-Later roadmap, Team Topologies' multi-team "platform grouping" pattern, and four named anti-patterns (rebranding, Field of Dreams Fallacy, Magpie Platform, centralising bottleneck) with observable early signals. 11 Key Findings, 3 high-confidence (two-or-more-independent-organisation corroboration), 8 medium-confidence (single-source). `learnings.md` Thread 18 updated with a new bullet extending the platform-as-bottleneck subordination-failure evidence.
